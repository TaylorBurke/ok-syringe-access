Viral Hepatitis Surveillance Report (2021 and 2023 editions)

U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, National Notifiable Diseases Surveillance System and National Vital Statistics System

Measures
Hepatitis C deaths by state 2017-2023 (Table 3.7), newly reported chronic hepatitis C by state (Table 3.5), acute hepatitis C by state (Table 3.1), risk exposures among acute cases (Table 3.3)
Caveat
Death rates are age-adjusted and count deaths with hepatitis C listed anywhere on the death certificate. Newly reported chronic cases depend on how much testing a state does, so a rise can mean more people were found, not more people infected. Acute cases are heavily under-reported and not comparable across states; we do not compare states on them. Texas does not report chronic hepatitis C.

Provisional drug overdose death counts (VSRR)

CDC National Center for Health Statistics

Measures
12-month-ending counts of drug overdose deaths by state of residence, monthly, 2015 to 2026
Caveat
Provisional and revised monthly; the latest months are flagged as underreported. We use CDC's predicted counts, which adjust for reporting delays (reported counts would exaggerate the recent decline), and compute rates with Census estimates.
